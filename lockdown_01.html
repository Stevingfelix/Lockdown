The Operating System

How Lockdown Runs.

You are placed in a Block of four other inmates. You do not pick them. We assign them. You will spend 168 days with these people whether you like them on Day 1 or not. By Day 60, you will know more about each other's businesses than your friends back home know about yours.

EVERY DAY
The Sit
Two hours of focused work on your one thing. Solo, with a Focusmate partner, or with one of your Block members. We do not care how. We care that it happens.
EVERY DAY
The Bell
You log your session in the daily channel. Hours worked, what you did, photo or screenshot proof. By 11:59pm. No log, no streak. We do not bend this rule.
WEDNESDAYS
Cell Watch
A 30-minute async or live check-in inside your Block. What you did since Sunday. What is stuck. What you will do by next Sunday. The Block runs this without me. I do not babysit cells.
BI-WEEKLY
Yard Time
75 minutes. I show up. I teach the next system in the playbook. Three inmates take the hot seat. Everyone leaves with a written commitment for the next 14 days.
MONDAY 8AM
The Ledger
Public scoreboard. Hours logged. Streak count. Ranked. Everyone sees where everyone stands. The single most uncomfortable, most clarifying piece of the whole system.
WEEK 12
The Half-Vow
Each inmate sits with where they actually are versus where they said they would be. One pivot is allowed, with written justification. After Week 12, your One Thing is locked. No more switching.
ONCE // ANYTIME
The Visitation
One 45-minute call with me. One-on-one. Voice or video. You request it anywhere in the 168 days. You decide when. You decide what for. You get one shot at sustained personal time with me. Use it on the inflection point that actually matters.

That is the entire operating system.

It is brutal in its simplicity.

It works because it is brutal in its simplicity.
