How Addicted To Starting Things Are You?

This is not a personality quiz. It is a diagnostic. By the end of it, you will have a number, a verdict, and an honest answer about whether the system that fixes the addiction was built for you or not.

Most Nigerians who start 14 things and finish zero believe they have a discipline problem. They do not. They have a structural problem.

This Intake measures the structural problem specifically. It scores you on 12 dimensions of the scattered-hustler pattern: how often you start, how predictably you quit, how consistently you blame the wrong thing, and what your relationship to finishing actually is.

You will get a score from 0 to 100, a verdict on which of four buckets you fall into, and a personalized diagnosis.

If you are addicted to starting things, this Intake will tell you. It will not lie to make you feel better.
